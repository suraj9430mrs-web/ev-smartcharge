ABOUT OUR COMPANY

Powering India's Electric Future

EV SmartCharge Technologies is building the backbone of sustainable electric mobility with intelligent, high-throughput charging hubs across key transit corridors and metro centers.

Our Vision & Mission

As millions of electric vehicles take to Indian roads, charging infrastructure must be as accessible, dependable, and swift as conventional refueling. EV SmartCharge was founded to solve charging congestion, eliminate queue confusion, and guarantee hardware compatibility for every electric vehicle driver.

Our network pairs heavy-duty DC fast-charging hardware (up to 150 kW) with an intelligent dispatch engine that automatically reserves the fastest available compatible slot for your car, drastically minimizing turnaround times and optimizing grid power consumption.

⚡

High-Throughput Charging

Deploying liquid-cooled dual-gun CCS-2 and CHAdeMO chargers providing up to 150 kW DC charging for passenger and fleet vehicles.

Fair Queueing Guarantee

99.5% Network SLA

Network Architecture & High-Performance Core

Our proprietary station allocation controller runs on an ultra-low-latency C++17 processing core. Arriving vehicles are matched to available chargers in real-time, computing estimated durations and logging complete audit telemetry for fleet operators and energy providers.
